Renter-Friendly Smart Security: The Best No-Drill Cameras & Smart Locks That Actually Work

Let’s talk about the unglamorous side of renting: the anxiety.

Maybe your apartment building has a buzzer that barely works. Maybe your front door faces a dimly lit hallway, or your ground-floor windows feel a little too accessible. You want peace of mind, but when you look at your lease, it clearly says: “No drilling, no changing locks, no altering the premises.”

So, how do you secure a space you don’t own?

Welcome to the world of renter-friendly smart security. Today’s technology has completely outpaced old-school landlord rules. You can now build a layered, professional-grade security system that requires zero screws, zero drilled holes, and zero permanent changes to your doors.

Here are the best no-drill smart security upgrades that will protect your space — and your security deposit.

1. The No-Drill Video Doorbell (That Actually Sticks)

The Vibe: Total porch awareness without the landlord’s permission.

The Upgrade: You don’t need to screw a Ring or Nest doorbell into your door frame. Many top-tier battery-powered video doorbells now come with (or can be paired with) heavy-duty, damage-free mounting kits.

These use industrial-strength 3M VHB (Very High Bond) tape that can withstand rain, snow, and extreme heat, but can be removed with a little heat from a hairdryer when you move out.

💡

Pro-Tip for Renters

If your landlord is strictly against any adhesive on the exterior door, buy a window mount kit. You can suction-cup or use a tiny, removable adhesive hook to mount the doorbell on the sidelight window next to your door, giving you the exact same viewing angle without touching the wood!

2. Smart Locks That Fit Over Your Existing Deadbolt

The Vibe: Keyless entry, auto-unlock, and keeping your original keys.

The Upgrade: Changing your physical deadbolt is a massive lease violation. But retrofit smart locks (like the August Wi-Fi Smart Lock or SwitchBot Lock) are the ultimate renter hack.

These brilliant devices install on the inside of your door, fitting directly over your existing thumb-turn. You simply slide it on, secure it with a few screws into the existing holes of your current lock, and snap the cover on. On the outside, your door looks completely unchanged. You still use your normal physical key, but now you can lock the door from your phone, give temporary digital keys to dog walkers, and auto-unlock when you walk up with groceries.

💡

Pro-Tip for Renters

When your lease is up, you just unsnap the smart lock, take it with you to your next apartment, and leave the original thumb-turn exactly as it was. Zero holes drilled, zero landlord anger.

3. Peel-and-Stick Window & Door Entry Sensors

The Vibe: Boutique-level security for your weakest points.

The Upgrade: Sliding glass doors and ground-floor windows are a renter’s biggest vulnerability. Smart entry sensors are tiny, two-piece devices that stick to the frame and the door/window itself. If the two pieces separate (meaning the door or window was opened), it instantly sends an alert to your phone.

They require no wiring and no screws. They just use strong, damage-free adhesive tape.

💡

Pro-Tip for Renters

Buy a multi-pack and put them everywhere. Stick one on your front door, your balcony slider, and even your medicine cabinet or liquor cabinet if you have roommates. You can also use them to alert you if your pet sneaks out the back door!

4. Plug-and-Play Indoor Cameras (With Damage-Free Mounting)

The Vibe: Keep an eye on your pets, your packages, and your space.

The Upgrade: You don’t need to mount indoor security cameras high up on the wall with drywall anchors. Modern smart cameras (like the Wyze Cam or Blink Mini) are small, beautiful, and designed to sit on a shelf or bookcase.

But if you want that high-angle “corner of the ceiling” view? Use damage-free adhesive hooks (like Command Hooks) or magnetic mounts. Many cameras have a magnetic base; you can stick a small adhesive metal plate to the top of your door frame or high on the wall, and the camera will snap right onto it.

💡

Pro-Tip for Renters

Hate the look of the USB power cord running down your wall? Use a paintable adhesive cord cover to hide the wire completely. It blends into your wall and peels off cleanly at move-out time.

5. Smart Lighting as a Security Deterrent

The Vibe: “Someone is definitely home.”

The Upgrade: The oldest security trick in the book is leaving a light on, but doing it manually is annoying. By pairing the smart lighting hacks from our Ultimate Guide to Renter-Friendly Smart Lighting with your security system, you create a powerful deterrent.

Set your smart bulbs or smart plugs to automatically turn on at sunset and turn off at bedtime. If you go out of town, use the “vacation mode” on your smart home app to randomize the lights. To anyone watching your apartment, it looks like you are home relaxing.

Secure Your Space, Keep Your Deposit

You shouldn’t have to choose between feeling safe in your home and getting your security deposit back. With these no-drill smart security upgrades, you can build a layered, high-tech defense system that travels with you from lease to lease.
